## Swiss angle

- No Swiss-domiciled (CH) global health care ETF was found. Swiss domicile mainly helps for Swiss stocks (35% Swiss withholding tax reclaimable); for a global fund, Irish domicile is the usual choice.
- Xtrackers XDWH is the only one with a **CHF trading line on SIX**, which avoids a currency conversion when buying.
- Roche and Novartis are included in all of these world health care funds.
