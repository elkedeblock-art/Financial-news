## Sector pulse
- **Gained favour:** Staples and utilities. Consumer Staples rose 3.8% and Utilities 2.9%, as investors sought safety. Swiss defensives like Nestlé, Novartis and Roche are the obvious link.

- **Lost favour:** Technology and communication services. Communication Services fell 4.5% and Technology 3.6%, on AI spending doubts.
